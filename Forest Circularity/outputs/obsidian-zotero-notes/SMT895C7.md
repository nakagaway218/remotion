# INFLUENCE OF DIGESTATE ON PHYSICAL  PROPERTIES OF SOIL IN ZD BUDIŠOV

## 書誌

- Authors: 
- Year: 2019
- Zotero key: SMT895C7

## 要約

Three variants of this experiment, one without digestate application, and two with digestate application, 1 × 20 t.ha–1 and 2 × 20 t.ha–1, were designed. Within a semi‐operational observation of selected physical properties of soil progress of penetrometer resistance, reduced bulk density and their influence on soil structure and water stability of structure aggregates were observed. From the acquired results, the research showed an increase in reduced bulk density (RBD) after digestate application which lead to decrease of soil porosity and minimal air capacity. Penetrometer resistance of soil showed analogically to RBD an increasing trend of compaction in variants with digestate application. Due to the impact of increase of penetrometer resistance of soil an increasing pedocompaction rate was observed. Control variant 1 without applied digestate showed a reversed trend. Furthermore, soil structure was affected negatively by the use of digestate. An observation of water aggregates stability shows a trend of deteriorating state from medium to low values in the variant with 2 × repeated digestate application. From the short‐term economic view, the yields of variants with digestate were higher due to higher amount of nutrients added into soil. The results thus indicate that plant yields tend to increase with digestate application but simultaneously, a risk of deterioration of parameters of physical properties of soil exists, which may further lead to irreversible soil degradation. In the company ZD Budišov the average digestate dose applied is 20 t.ha–1. A higher dose of digestate is applied on farmland to decompose straw. Biogas station with output of 1 MW produces 25,000 t of digestate annually. A correct application and compliance with technological process of application enables use of digestate in the agricultural business as fertilizer while maintaining minimal changes of production properties of soil. It is desirable effective biogas production in biogas stations as well as digestate production had minimal impact on agricultural production of a given agricultural business.  R
